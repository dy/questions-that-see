# 🎯 PROJECT — WHERE TO GO?

*Look forward. Arrow. Direction and movement.*

---

## 1. WHAT PAIN AM I HERE TO END—EXACTLY?

- One person. One moment. One curse lifted. Show me.
- What would they whisper to a friend about this problem?
- What silent prayer does this answer?
- If I can't name the pain—do I know what I'm building?

---

## 2. WHAT'S THE ONE JOB—NO ESCAPE?

- If it could only do ONE thing—what survives the fire?
- What does victory taste like? Feel like?
- What's the one metric that defines success?
- Can I say it in 7 words or less?

---

## 3. WHERE DOES THIS END—WHAT'S OUT?

- What must it NOT become? What would betray it?
- What feature would users beg for that I must refuse?
- What's bone vs flesh? What's essential vs ornament?
- What would I cut if I had half the time?

---

## 4. WHAT'S THE SOUL—THE THING ONLY I CAN SEE?

- What makes this alive, not just useful?
- What can't be copied? Where's the magic?
- What would make someone say "whoa"?
- Where is competition irrelevant?

---

## 5. WHAT LIE AM I TELLING MYSELF?

- What assumption breaks under pressure?
- What technical limit am I ignoring?
- What disaster am I pretending won't happen?
- Is this offering—or ego dressed as service?
- Would I still build this if no one ever knew?

---

## 6. WHAT DECISION UNLOCKS ALL THE OTHERS?

- What am I avoiding deciding?
- What would I choose if fear left the room?
- What limitation—introduced or removed—would unblock everything?
- Reversible → move fast. Irreversible → sleep on it. Which is this?
- What's the ONE commitment that makes everything else easier?

---

## 7. WHAT'S THE NEXT MOVE—NOT STRATEGY, THE MOVE?

- What would I ship if I had to ship TODAY?
- What's the smallest thing that's genuinely useful?
- What's the 20% that delivers 80%?
- What single action can I take in the next 30 minutes?

---

## 8. WHAT AM I WAITING FOR—PERMISSION?

- What would I do if I knew I couldn't fail?
- What would I do if I knew I WOULD fail?
- What's the cost of delay? What window is closing?
- What's stopping me that isn't actually real?

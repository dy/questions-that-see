QUESTIONS THAT SEE — Combat Page

1. INTENTION
	•	Am I here to serve — or to look good?
	•	Would I do this if no one ever knew it was me?
	•	Is this an offering — or a transaction?
	•	What part of my ego is trying to sneak in?

⸻

2. THE PAIN
	•	Whose pain am I solving?
	•	What is actually hurting them right now?
	•	What frustrates or blocks them?
	•	What would they complain about to a friend?
	•	What stops hurting when this exists?

⸻

3. THE VALUE
	•	Why would anyone care?
	•	What would they miss if this disappeared?
	•	What does this make possible?
	•	What is real here — not decoration?

⸻

4. THE CORE
	•	If this could do only one thing, what must it do perfectly?
	•	Without what does it collapse?
	•	What is essential — and what is just nice to have?

⸻

5. THE BOUNDARY
	•	What is this not?
	•	What must we refuse even if users ask?
	•	What would dilute or betray this?

⸻

6. THE RISK
	•	What must be true for this to work?
	•	What assumption could kill it?
	•	What am I pretending is easy?
	•	What looks good but is actually fake progress?

⸻

7. THE MOVE
	•	What is the smallest thing I can ship that is actually useful?
	•	What would I ship if I had to ship today?
	•	What 20% creates 80% of the value?

⸻

8. THE TEST
	•	How will I know this is working?
	•	What is the first real signal?
	•	What would prove me wrong fast?

⸻

9. THE SMELL
	•	What feels off, ugly, or clumsy?
	•	What would a craftsman fix first?
	•	What single flaw, if fixed, would improve everything?

⸻

10. THE TRUTH
	•	Would I use this myself?
	•	Would I recommend it to someone I respect?
	•	Would I pay for it?

⸻

11. THE BLINDSPOT
	•	What am I not seeing?
	•	What am I avoiding?
	•	What uncomfortable truth is here?

⸻

12. TIME
	•	What about this will embarrass me in 6 months?
	•	What would make this timeless instead?

⸻

THE RAZOR

Before you ship, answer:
	1.	What suffering ends here?
	2.	One job — done perfectly?
	3.	What am I not seeing?
	4.	Will this embarrass me in 6 months?
	5.	Is this an offering — or an ego trip?

⸻

Ship what matters.
Delete what doesn’t.
Serve — don’t perform.

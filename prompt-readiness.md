# 🎁 **READINESS** — *Is it complete? Ready to offer?*

> Truth > Users > Developers > Agents > Ecosystem
> — adapted from W3C Priority of Constituencies

**Ready when**: Adding more would make it worse. Removing anything would make it incomplete.

---

## 1. **Truth** — *Why does this exist?*

- What exact pain does this heal? *(One sentence.)*
- Is it honest? Does it claim only what it delivers?
- Is it complete? Nothing half-done, nothing excess?
- Will it last? Stable foundations, minimal dependencies?
- Is it elegant? Simple core, complex only where necessary?

⚡ Would you offer this to God?

---

## 2. **Users** — *Can they succeed without help?*

- Can they start in 5 minutes? Install, run, see it work?
- Does it do what it promises?
- Do errors help them recover?
- Are defaults sensible?

⚡ Follow your own README. Does it work?

---

## 3. **Developers** — *Can they contribute without asking?*

- Can they find things? Intuitive structure?
- Can they understand the code? Names reveal intent?
- Can they verify changes? Tests exist and pass?
- Is everything in the repo? No hidden knowledge?

⚡ Could a stranger maintain this if you disappeared?

---

## 4. **Agents** — *Can AI work here autonomously?*

- Are instructions clear? README sufficient?
- Is feedback fast? Lint/test under 2 minutes?
- Are patterns consistent? Similar problems, similar solutions?
- Is the code self-documenting?

⚡ Could Claude complete a task without asking questions?

---

## 5. **Ecosystem** — *Does it play well with others?*

- Does it follow conventions?
- Does it integrate cleanly?
- Is the environment clear?
- Is metadata complete?

⚡ Would an expert in this ecosystem approve?

---

## The Hard Questions

1. What pain does this actually heal?
2. What would a harsh critic see in 5 seconds?
3. What are you avoiding or hiding from?
4. What removal would improve everything?
5. Is this ready to offer to the world?

No comfort. Brutal honesty. *(Answer them.)*

# 🔮 EXPLORE — WHAT WANTS TO EXIST?

*Open wide. Discover. Before committing—survey.*

---

## 1. WHO ACHES—AND WHAT'S THEIR IDEAL DAY?

*Name the person. See their pain. Then see the magic.*

- One person. Name them. What's their frustrated Tuesday?
- What do they complain to a friend? What's their silent prayer?
- What's the emotion—frustration? shame? exhaustion?
- **Painful now**: Walk through their struggle. Each friction.
- **Magic future**: If this worked perfectly, what's their day?
- The gap = the job to do.

---

## 2. WHAT TERRITORY AM I ENTERING?

*Survey the landscape. Find the gap. Spot the blue ocean.*

- What already exists? Best in class—why? Worst—what killed it?
- What's everyone doing that I should avoid? What's the trap?
- What obvious solution is everyone ignoring? Why?
- What do incumbents assume that isn't true anymore?
- What category does this create—or belong to? Or destroy?
- **Blue ocean**: Where does competition become irrelevant?

---

## 3. WHERE ELSE COULD THIS LIVE?

*Cross-pollinate. Transplant the seed.*

- What's the obvious application? The unusual one?
- What if this served a completely different audience?
- What's the "X for Y" that opens unexpected doors?
- What field has the answer we're missing?

---

## 4. WHAT EXCITES—WHAT DRAINS?

*Energy as compass. Follow life, not duty.*

- What about this feels like play? What feels like slog?
- What would I explore even if it failed?
- What am I forcing that doesn't want to move?
- Where's the spark? Chase that.

---

## 5. STOP. IS THIS REAL?

*Scary questions. Mind is Krishna's resource—don't waste it.*

- **Is anyone actually waiting—or am I building my fantasy?**
- Who asked for this? Name them. Can't? Stop.
- What would the world lose if this never existed? (Nothing? Walk away.)
- What technical reality am I ignoring? What's actually hard?
- What disaster am I pretending won't happen?
- Am I exploring—or hiding from shipping?
- Is this intellectual tourism disguised as research?
- Would I still build this if no one ever knew it was mine?
- **What would my enemy see in 5 seconds?**

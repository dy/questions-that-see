# 🔥 PROJECT STATE RAZOR

*6 questions. No hiding. Cut through.*

---

## 1. IS THIS AN OFFERING—OR ARE YOU SHOWING OFF?

- Would my spiritual master approve? Would Krishna smile?
- Service or performance? Love or show?
- Would I do this if no one ever knew?
- What remains when applause disappears?

---

## 2. WHAT'S ALIVE HERE—AND WHAT'S ALREADY DEAD?

- What EXCITES? *(Chase it—that's the signal.)*
- What DRAINS? *(Kill it—that's the poison.)*
- What feels like play vs slog?
- What would I do even if it failed?

---

## 3. WHAT AM I PRETENDING NOT TO KNOW?

- What decision am I afraid to make?
- What truth hurts too much to admit?
- What would I do if fear left the room?
- What wants to die so something better can live?

---

## 4. WHAT TREASURE AM I SITTING ON BLIND?

- What blessing hides as obstacle?
- What's already complete that I'm over-complicating?
- What would an outsider see instantly that I miss?
- What "aha" is waiting to happen?

---

## 5. WHAT WILL MAKE ME CRINGE IN 6 MONTHS?

- What's the simplest thing that could work?
- What am I over-engineering out of fear?
- What would I cut if launch was tomorrow?
- Good enough NOW vs perfect NEVER?

---

## 6. WHAT STINKS—AND WHY AM I PRETENDING IT DOESN'T?

- What's ugly, clunky, slow, confusing?
- What would my harshest critic see in 5 seconds?
- What assumption breaks under pressure?
- What ONE fix would fix many?

---

## 7. WILL THIS LAST—OR IS IT NOISE?

- What makes this timeless vs trendy?
- What would make it canonical? Classic? Referenced for years?
- What principle here transcends the moment?
- What would I be proud of in 10 years?

---

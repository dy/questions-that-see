# QUESTIONS THAT SEE

*Contemplation for work worth doing*

---

# ∞ INTENTION

**Serving or performing?**
Is this for God or for myself?
Would I do this if no one ever knew?
Who will this help?

**What am I trying to prove?**
What wound am I covering?
What remains if applause disappears?

**Would this qualify as an offering?**
Would I be proud to show my teacher?

---

# 1. PAIN

**Whose pain — and where's the root?**
Show me the exact moment it occurs.
What would they complain about to a friend?
What keeps them up at night?

**What stops hurting?**
What door opens?
Who would cry if this disappeared?

**Who is NOT our person?**

---

# 2. TERRITORY

**What already exists?**
What obvious solution is everyone ignoring?
What traps to avoid?

**Why now?**
What wants to be born through this moment?

**What's the magic?**
What can't be copied?
What could someone love about this?
What would make them say "whoa"?

---

# 3. ESSENCE

**If it could only do ONE thing — what?**
What must be perfect, everything else forgiven?
How will I know it's working?

**Where's the boundary?**
What must this NOT become?
What's bone, what's flesh?

**Where's the weak point?**
What breaks first?

**Every yes is a no. What am I trading?**

**What decision unlocks all others?**
What would I decide if fear wasn't here?

---

# 4. ACTION

**What's hiding in plain sight?**
What must be true for this to work?
What looks good but is actually fake?

**What would I ship TODAY?**
What gives maximum for minimum?
What's already done that I'm not seeing?

**No deadline = no ship. What's the date?**
One hour — what's the one thing?

**What feels like play? What feels like slog?**
Where's the flow? Follow it.

**What am I afraid to ship?**
Eyes fear — hands do.

---

# 5. REVIEW

**Would I use this? Pay for it?**

**If we deleted half — which half lives?**
What would a strict editor remove?

**What smells wrong?**
What would a craftsman fix first?
What one flaw, fixed, fixes many?

**What's symptom, what's cause?**

**What mistake keeps returning?**
What pattern owns me?
What would I confess if I were honest?

**What will embarrass me in 6 months?**

---

# ∞ BLINDSPOTS

**What am I conveniently ignoring?**
What disaster am I pretending won't happen?

**What's my favorite illusion?**
What failure am I romanticizing?
What if the obstacle IS the way?

**What question am I dodging?**
Is this even the right problem?

**What would my enemy see in 5 seconds?**
What's too obvious to notice?

---

# ∞ GOLD

**What gift hides inside the limitation?**
What's in plain sight that everyone misses?

**What if this is about something else?**
What would make this legendary?

**What would a child see?**
What would grandmother understand immediately?
What's obvious to beginner but invisible to expert?

**What does gut know that analysis doesn't?**
What would I do if I trusted myself?

**What's this really about?**
Who will this help?

---

# RAZOR

1. **What suffering ends here?**
2. **One job — done perfectly?**
3. **What am I not seeing?**
4. **Embarrassing in 6 months?**
5. **Would I be proud to show my teacher?**

---

*Serve, don't perform.*
*The obstacle is the way.*
*Trust yourself.*
